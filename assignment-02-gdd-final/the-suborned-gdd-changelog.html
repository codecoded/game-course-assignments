REVIEW

What review changed

All three reviews were of the submitted v0.9.18. A peer helped talk through the game concepts. The other two led to the changes below.

Course instructor: less manual, more game

The GDD read too much like a manual. It needed to show the game and the excitement of how it plays.

ProblemChange in v0.12.0
The build led; the game came later.Sections 01–08 are what you play. The build and the agent crew come after. In the 6-page submission, pages 1–4 are the game.
Too technical for a design document.Plain language throughout. Schemas, file formats and the test plan moved to the agentic design document.
Agent names were confusing.The crew is listed by job first, name second (Systems designer · Motive).

AI reviewer on GitHub: four gaps in the rules

Fixed in v0.10.18 and still in place in v0.12.0.

GapFix
The proof carried too much; its goal and ending were hard to test.One focused mission with an explicit win and fail.
Time pressure had no source.Each mission has a closing event: Vale leaves at handover.
Nothing in the city pushed back except the player.A rival crew pursues its own goal on the same clock.
An action couldn't be traced to its result.Every event states what it needs, what it changes and who sees it. Any run replays exactly.
